About Data Deletion

In accordance with applicable data protection regulations and Meta Platform Terms, Capricon CRM provides users with the ability to request the deletion of their personal data. When you remove the Capricon CRM app from your Facebook account, this URL is called by Meta to notify us of your data deletion request.

What Data We Store

When you connect your Meta (Facebook) account to Capricon CRM, we may store the following information:

  • Facebook Page ID and Page Name
  • Page Access Token (used to retrieve leads)
  • Lead information synced from your Facebook Lead Ads (name, email, phone number)

How to Request Data Deletion

You can request data deletion through any of the following methods:

1

Via Facebook Settings

Go to your Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites → find Capricon CRM → click Remove. This will automatically trigger a data deletion request to us.

2

Disconnect in App

Navigate to the Meta Leads section in the Capricon CRM admin panel and click Disconnect to unlink your Facebook account.

3

Email Us Directly

Send a data deletion request email to developer.inspitetech@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

What Happens After Deletion

Upon receiving your data deletion request:

  • Your Facebook connection details (Page ID, access token, page name) will be removed from our system
  • Any synced lead data will be permanently deleted from our database
  • The deletion process will be completed within 30 days of your request
  • You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete

Check Deletion Status

If you've already submitted a data deletion request, you can check the status by contacting us with your confirmation code at developer.inspitetech@gmail.com.
